# Three of Cups

Two became a bond; now a third arrives, and the bond becomes a circle. This is the number of form — the impulse, once felt, at last takes shape and is understood — and here it takes the shape of a toast raised in summer, three vessels lifted and touching in the air. Mercury runs quick through the waters of Cancer, carrying good news up the road to the house where the family waits: a birth, a betrothal, a wedding, a medal pinned to a coat, a book finished and passed hand to hand. Binah in Briah — the great Mother pouring herself into the world of creation, giving her fluid abundance a cup to be held in.

The lesson of the card is that joy, to exist at all, must be shared. Feeling kept private evaporates; feeling poured out becomes a festival. Dedication ripens into certainty, certainty into celebration. Generosity is not a decoration on happiness — it is the container.

But water that only circles a single vessel grows stale and sweet. The shadow of this card is the party that never ends, the mirror mistaken for a friend: narcissism, hedonism, decadence, appetite dressed in garlands.

Turned over, the wine spills. The gathering is postponed, the honor withheld or wrongly given, the wish rejected at the door. Here are dashed hopes and domestic crisis, divorce and miscarriage, sickness in the family, the friendship broken over something small and unforgiven. Its meanness is the miser's: envy where there should be gladness, credit withheld, compassion rationed, warmth spent as though there were not enough to go around.

Within weeks the answer comes. Ask yourself, before it does, whether your hands are open — and whose cup you have neglected to fill.
