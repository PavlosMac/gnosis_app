# Five of Disks

Mercury, quick and calculating, has wandered into the slow green pastures of Taurus and found nothing there to count. This is Geburah in the world of substance — severity striking the settled form, the fifth number arriving as it always does, to test what four had built and to break it open. Spring is on the earth, but the body inside the coat does not feel it.

Here is worry in its purest chemistry: the mind circling the ledger at three in the morning, the arithmetic that never resolves. Debts with teeth in them, taxes, judgments, a livelihood withdrawn like a tide. Assets scatter; skills feel too few or too old for the market; the purse empties faster than it fills. And beneath the practical hardship runs the deeper poverty — the suspicion that one is not worth much, which makes it hard to give, hard to receive, hard to ask. Exploitation finds such people easily. So does resentment, that acid which corrodes the vessel holding it.

The card can speak of the body too, of the illness or the habit long since advanced, the toxicity accumulated in slow earth-time: poor diet, depression, addiction, energies gone moribund from repetition. Cold is the condition, and cold makes us calculating. Some of the walls in this landscape are self-built, and that is the hardest news the card carries — some doors were never locked.

Turned over, the freeze breaks. Feeling returns to the extremities: sensitivity, empathy, honesty, an unclenching. A skill is learned that someone will pay for. The long season of fear releases its grip, and prosperity begins its slow return — for disks measure time in seasons, not moments. What is restored here is not only money but moral rectitude, and the willingness, at last, to give.
