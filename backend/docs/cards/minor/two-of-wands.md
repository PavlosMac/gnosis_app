# Two of Wands

**Dominion.** Mars rules the first ten degrees of Aries, and here the spark of spring strikes the tinder of the year — the raw fire of Chokmah, wisdom before it has taken a body, force before it has found its form. The static period ends. Something in you that has been coiled and waiting steps out of the planning room into the open air.

Two is the number of the first outward move: one flame divides and now there are two directions, two ventures, two hands on the same rope. This is the card of ignition and debut, of accepting the challenge, of the competitor who discovers that the game itself is a pleasure. It favours confederation — the joining of wills, the diplomatic handshake that doubles the heat of the furnace. Days, not years; the wands burn fast.

But dominion is a heavy word, and Mars is not a patient god. Two fires can warm a house or set it alight. The same doubling that offers alliance can offer division: the battle of wills, the false ally whose loyalty thins as the work grows real, the ambition aimed at the wrong horizon. Haste skips the details. Indecision hovers between the two paths and burns the fuel of both.

Reversed, the flame gutters. Enthusiasm leaks away, commitment is given only in part, projects are abandoned before they prove themselves, or they prove themselves for somebody else. The collaboration sours; suspicion and ego take the place of shared heat. Here the card counsels stillness — do not begin, do not combine, do not pour resolve into a vessel that leaks.

Upright, ask yourself only this: which of the two deserves your fire? Then strike.
