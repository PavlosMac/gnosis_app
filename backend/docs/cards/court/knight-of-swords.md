# Knight of Swords

Fire riding air: a storm-front of a man, lightning given a horse and a direction. He is Chokmah in the world of formation — raw will pouring itself into intellect, the first flash before the thought has cooled into language. Where he appears, expect brilliance at speed. He is the scientist, the engineer, the philosopher who runs, the mind that dismantles a problem in the time others need to describe it. Precise, original, indefatigable, honest to the point of shock, he inspires because he does not hesitate.

But fire in air burns clean and cold. The same blade that cuts to truth cuts people. Throned upon the nine of swords — the card of night terrors — he can lie awake calculating, arrogant and unforgiving, judging where he might have listened, wielding secrets rather than sharing them. Cleverness without warmth becomes cruelty wearing a reasonable face.

Overturned, the horse falls. The gallop becomes stumbling: scattered attention, mediocrity, procrastination, judgment impaired and evidence faulty. Advisers prove incompetent, witnesses mistaken, news bad. What was quicksilver goes dull.

He may be an actual man, past thirty-six, sharp-tongued and gifted. He may be your own mind at full charge. Ride him — but keep the reins.
