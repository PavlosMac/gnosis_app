# Knight of Wands

Fire within fire — the flame that needs no kindling, only a direction. This is Chokmah in the world of pure emanation: raw will turned upon itself, the lightning-flash of first impulse before thought has time to argue. He is the man past thirty-six who has stopped asking permission, throned on the hard-won ground of the Nine, ruler of the arrows in flight.

When he moves through a reading, something begins. A project, an adventure, a leaving-behind. He has vision and the nerve to act on it, the practicality to make the leap land, the resilience to rise again. He competes, he masters, he breaks the boundary others merely study. In him a life becomes meaningful because it is spent on something.

But a furnace unattended burns the house. His conviction hardens into opinion, his drive into remorselessness; he inflates, mistakes his appetite for destiny, and cannot rest — cannot even conceive of rest — because failure is unthinkable and therefore always near.

Inverted, the flame gutters. Doubt, hesitation, surrender before the contest is decided. The mask falls and reveals borrowed courage: a man easily led, dependent, dishonoured in his own eyes. Or such a man arrives in your life, all conflict and hollow bluster.

Ask, then: is your fire aimed, or merely hot?
