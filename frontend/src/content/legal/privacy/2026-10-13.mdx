## 2. What personal data we process and why

Your email address, name and password are required to create an account. Without them we cannot provide the service. Everything else below is created as you use it.

- **Email address.** Used to identify your account, sign you in, send you account emails such as confirmation and password-reset links, and answer support requests. Legal basis: performance of the contract you enter by accepting the Terms. Kept until you delete your account.
- **First and last name.** Used to address you in the service and in support correspondence. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Kept until you delete your account.
- **Password and account credentials.** Used to authenticate you and provide access to your account. Passwords are stored securely and are not available to us in readable form. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Kept until you delete your account or change your password.
- **Readings.** The spread, the cards, your question, your tags and, for significator charts, the birth date you enter. Used to provide and save your readings. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Kept until you delete your account.
- **AI interpretations.** The text generated for each reading. Used to give you the interpretation you asked for and let you read it again. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Kept until you delete your account.
- **Usage and cost records.** How much of the interpretation service your account has used. Used to apply the usage budget on your account. Legal basis: performance of the contract and our legitimate interest in controlling the cost of the service. Kept until you delete your account.
- **Agreement and acceptance records.** Which version of the Terms and of this Policy you accepted, and when. Used to show what you agreed to. Legal basis: our legal obligation to be able to demonstrate it. Kept until you delete your account.
- **Temporary verification and password-reset information.** Used to verify your email address and help you regain access to your account. This information is retained only for as long as necessary for these purposes. Legal basis: performance of the contract.
- **Security and abuse-prevention information.** We may temporarily process information associated with requests such as registration, sign-in, password resets and support requests, including your network address, to protect accounts and the service against abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in maintaining the security of the service. Kept only briefly.
- **Session information.** We process information necessary to maintain your signed-in session and allow you to sign out or otherwise end active sessions. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Kept for up to 30 days, or until you sign out or reset your password.

If you register but do not confirm your email address, your account and associated data are automatically deleted after 7 days, or 7 days after the most recent confirmation link was requested.

## 3. Cookies and local storage

**Necessary cookies.** We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, maintain your session and securely renew your authentication. These cookies are necessary for the service to function and are not used for advertising.

**Local storage.** We also store a small amount of interface state in your browser's local storage, such as a display preference and how many readings and significator charts you have drawn without an account. This information remains on your device and is not sent to us.


## 4. Recipients, processors and international transfers

We share personal data only with the following providers, and only as far as their task requires:

- **OpenAI** generates the interpretations. **What you enter for a reading is sent to OpenAI**: the spread, the cards, your question and, for significator charts, the birth date. Your name and email address are not sent. Avoid putting information in a question that you would not want to share, especially about your health or about other people.
- **Resend** delivers our emails. It receives your email address and the content of the emails we send you.

Both providers process data in the United States under a data-processing agreement with us. Transfers outside the EU and EEA are protected as follows: our agreement with OpenAI is with OpenAI Ireland Limited and incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; Resend participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its agreement also incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to:

- **access** your data and receive a copy of it;
- have inaccurate data **corrected**;
- have your data **erased**. You can do this yourself: **Delete account** on your profile removes your account and all data linked to it immediately and permanently;
- **restrict** or **object to** processing based on our legitimate interests;
- **data portability**;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent;
- **lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live. Our supervisory authority is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten), Box 8114, 104 20 Stockholm, Sweden, imy@imy.se, [www.imy.se](https://www.imy.se).

To exercise a right other than erasure, use the **Contact support** form on your profile. We respond within 30 days.

You can review the documents you have accepted at any time under **Agreements** on your profile. If you no longer accept the Terms, you can log out or delete your account. There is no penalty beyond losing access to the service that requires them.

## 6. Automated decision-making and AI

Interpretations are generated automatically by an AI model and are not reviewed by a person before you see them. They are text for your reflection. They produce no legal effect on you and no similarly significant effect, and we make no decisions about you based on them.

## 7. Security

We protect your data with measures appropriate to the service. Passwords are stored in a form we cannot read. Sign-in links sent by email can be used only once. Connections to the site are encrypted, and sessions can be ended by signing out or resetting your password.

## 8. Children

The service is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.
