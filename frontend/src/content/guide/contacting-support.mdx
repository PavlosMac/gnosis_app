If something is not working or you have a question, you can write to the team from your account settings. You need to be signed in.

1. Open your profile from the ☥ menu, then press **Account settings** in the left panel.
2. Press **Contact support**. A window titled **Seek Counsel** opens.

3. Fill in **Subject** ("How can we help?") and **Message** ("Describe your question or issue..."). The subject can be up to 200 characters and the message up to 5000.
4. Press **Send**.

When it works you will see "Your message has been sent. The keeper of the sanctum will reply to your email." The reply comes to the email address on your account, so check that it is the one you read.

If you see "Session expired. Please log in again.", sign in and send it again.

You can also reach **Contact support** from the **Updated Terms** page, if you are stuck there.
