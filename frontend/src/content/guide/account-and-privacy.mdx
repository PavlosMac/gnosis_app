Your agreements and your account are managed from your profile.

## Review your agreements

On your profile press **Account settings**, then under **Agreements** press **Review / change**.

The page shows the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy, with the version and the date you accepted each. Open either one to read it in full. Press **Save** after ticking a box you have not yet accepted.

Unticking a box does not withdraw your acceptance. To decline, log out or delete your account.

## Reset your password

On your profile press **Account settings**, then **Reset Password**. A reset link is sent to your email: "A reset link has been sent to your email."

## Delete your account

On your profile press **Account settings**, then **Delete account** at the bottom of the page.

  src="/guide/account-and-privacy/02-delete.png"

This cannot be undone. It immediately and permanently removes:

- your account details and sign-in
- every saved reading, its interpretation and your tags
- your usage record and your agreement records

To confirm, enter your **Password** and type DELETE, then press **Delete My Account**. You are taken back to the front page. Press **Keep my account** if you change your mind.
