The Oracle can write an interpretation of a saved reading, reading your cards together in their positions. You need an account and a saved reading.

## Two ways to ask

You can ask straight after the reading, or come back to it later from your journal.

### Straight after your reading

Once a reading is saved, **Oracle Interpretation** appears under it.

Press it and the Oracle starts at once. There is no confirmation step, so press it only when you are ready.

### Later, from your journal

Open the reading from your [Readings Journal](/guide/finding-readings). If it has no interpretation yet, the same **Oracle Interpretation** button is under the cards. This time a window first asks "Ready to consult the Oracle for this reading?", and nothing is sent until you press **Consult the Oracle**.

## While it works

Wait while the window shows "The Oracle consults the stars...". A large reading can take a little while. Then read the interpretation and press **Done**. You are taken to the reading's page, where the interpretation stays for good.

## Good to know

- **One interpretation per reading.** It cannot be regenerated, so frame your question with care, see [Reading the Cards](/guide/reading-the-cards).
- **The chalice.** Each interpretation uses part of your Oracle budget. The window ends with "Remaining Oracle budget", and your profile shows it as the Remaining Readings chalice. When the budget is used up you will see "Your Oracle budget is exhausted."
- **If something goes wrong** the window offers **Try Again**. If your session has expired it offers **Log In** instead.
- **Updated terms.** If you are asked to accept updated Terms and Privacy Policy, open your profile and review them first.
