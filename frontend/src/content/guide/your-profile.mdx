## The Tally

The left panel is about your account.

- **Remaining Readings** is a chalice: the fuller it is, the more Oracle interpretations you have left. When it says "The chalice runs dry" you can no longer request interpretations.
- **Account settings** opens a page with **Contact support** (see [Contacting Support](/guide/contacting-support)), **Agreements**, which shows whether you have accepted the Terms and Conditions and read the Privacy Policy, **Reset Password**, which emails you a reset link, and **Delete account**. Agreements and deletion are covered in [Account and Privacy](/guide/account-and-privacy).

## The Readings Journal

The right panel summarises your saved readings.

- The large number is how many readings you have saved.
- **Last Reading** shows the spread, date and question of your latest reading, and whether it has been interpreted. Select it to open it.
- **Focused** lists your most used tags. Select a tag to open your readings filtered by it.
- The two round buttons at the bottom start a **New reading** or open **All readings**.

## Your significators

Significators are the cards that represent you, worked out from your birth date. They are on their own page, [Significators](/significators), which does not need an account.

Enter your day, month and year and press **Generate Significators**. You will see your Day Number, Astrological Sign, Life Numbers, Decanate and Court Royal cards, each with its meaning.
