Open the [Reading Oracle](/reading), or press **Oracle** on the front page. You do not need an account to draw cards, but you do need one to save the reading.

1. Under **Choose Your Reading**, pick a spread. Each option shows its number of cards, for example "Past, Present, Future (3 Cards)". The spreads are Single Card, (Past, Present, Future), (Fire, Water, Air, Earth), Two Card, Three Card, Relationship Reading, Career Reading, Tree of Life and Significators.
2. Select **Spread Layout** to see where each card will fall.
3. Choose **Upright Only** or **With Reversals**. With reversals, some cards will turn up upside down, see [Reading the Cards](/guide/reading-the-cards). Note the 'Significators' represent a chart and are not reveresed.
4. For Single Card, Two Card and Three Card, 'Open' type question. Input the question before they can begin. It helps focus the reading and will be saved with the reading if you choose so later.
5. For the Relationship Reading you can name the two people. Unlock a pillar to replace "Querent" and "Other" with real names. Default is Querent (user/you) and Other.
6. Press **Begin Your Journey**.

The Significators spread asks for a birth date instead of drawing cards.

## Draw your cards

The Oracle shuffles automatically. Then 78 face-down cards appear with the instruction "Select N card(s) from the Rider Waite deck".

Select cards one at a time. They turn over as you choose them. When you have chosen them all, the reading appears on its own.

Each card sits in a named position. Read them together, as described in [Reading the Cards](/guide/reading-the-cards). To keep the reading, see [Saving a Reading](/guide/saving-a-reading). To start over, press **New Reading**.

<Callout title="Reading with your own deck">
If you read with a physical deck, you can record the result yourself on the Manual Reading page, linked from your Readings Journal. You choose each card, set it upright or reversed, then reveal and save the reading.
</Callout>
