Saving puts a reading in your **Readings Journal** so you can return to it, tag it and ask the Oracle about it. You need to be signed in.

## Save it

<Shot
  src="/guide/saving-a-reading/01-save-icon.png"
  w={1280}
  h={500}
  alt="The bookmark icon at the top right of a finished reading"
  marks={[
    { x: 86, y: 4, w: 11, h: 24, label: "Save reading" },
  ]}
/>

1. Finish a reading, see [Making a Reading](/guide/making-a-reading).
2. Press the bookmark icon at the top right of the reading, labelled **Save reading**. It reads "Saving..." for a moment.
3. When it is done the icon fills in and shows **Saved to Journal**.

There is no form to fill in: the spread, the cards and any question you asked are saved as they were. If you are not signed in, the bookmark asks you to log in first, then keeps your reading.

A saved reading appears in your [Readings Journal](/guide/finding-readings). The Relationship Reading is tagged automatically with the two names you gave it.

## Add tags

Tags let you group readings, for example "career" or "love". You add them on the reading's own page, which you reach from your journal.

<Shot
  src="/guide/saving-a-reading/02-tags.png"
  w={1280}
  h={800}
  alt="A saved reading with its tags"
  marks={[
    { x: 30, y: 14, w: 40, h: 9, label: "Existing tags and the + button" },
    { x: 30, y: 26, w: 40, h: 9, label: "Type a tag, then confirm with the tick or cancel with the cross" },
  ]}
/>

1. Open the reading from your journal.
2. Press the **+** beside the tags.
3. Type your tag in the box ("Add a tag…") and press the tick to save, or the cross to cancel.

A reading can have up to five tags. When you reach five, the box reads "Max 5 tags".
