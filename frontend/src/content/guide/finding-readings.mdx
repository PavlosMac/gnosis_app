## Reading cards

Each entry shows the spread, the date, your question, any tags and the number of cards. Select an entry to open the full reading, including its interpretation if it has one. On a reading's page, the arrows move to the previous or next reading, and **All Readings** takes you back.

## Filter your readings

Press **Filter Readings** to open the panel. It opens by itself when a filter is active.

1. **Spread Type**: choose one spread, or leave it on "Any Spread". Select a chosen spread again to remove it.
2. **Tags**: start typing under "Type to search tags…" and pick from the suggestions, which show how many readings use each tag. You can add several.
3. **Birth Date**: available only when the spread type is Significators.
4. Press **Apply**. **Clear** removes every filter.

You can also reach a filtered list from your profile by selecting a tag under **Focused**.

## Sort and page through

- **Newest first** and **Oldest first** is one link that flips the order. It appears when you have more than one reading.
- **Per page** lets you show 10, 20 or 50 readings at a time once you have more than 10.
- Use **Prev** and **Next** at the bottom to move between pages.

<Callout title="Read with your own deck?">
Below the list, **Record that reading here** takes you to the Manual Reading page, where you enter the cards from a physical deck and save them to your journal.
</Callout>
